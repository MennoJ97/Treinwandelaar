# AI training crawlers are not welcome; AI *search* crawlers are (OAI-SearchBot,
# ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot and the like fetch

# Applebot-Extended are not crawlers but opt-out tokens: Googlebot and Applebot
# still crawl for search, they just may not use the pages for training.

# the whole rule for these names. The same list is in the robots.txt of all
# five indexed sites on mhjansen.nl; keep them in step.
